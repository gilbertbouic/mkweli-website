FAQ

Straight answers for compliance teams

Is Mkweli a full AML compliance system?

No. It is a focused sanctions name-screening tool. You still need policies, risk assessment, transaction monitoring, SAR processes, and governance appropriate to your licence and regulator. Always verify critical hits against primary official sources before blocking or reporting.

Does it require internet?

Screening works offline with lists stored on the device. Internet is only needed to download the APK and to refresh lists from official public feeds.

Which lists are included?

United States OFAC SDN, United Nations Security Council consolidated list, European Union financial sanctions, and the United Kingdom FCDO sanctions list. Counts change as those lists change; the app shows live entry counts after each successful update.

How does name matching work?

v1.0.12 uses token matching (name parts, any order) with ranked strong and possible scores. It is not phonetic fuzzy matching and not a full KYC platform.

Is the app free and who publishes it?

Yes — free Android APK, no per-seat fee. Published by Mkweli (mkweli.tech). Source: github.com/gilbertbouic/MkweliMobile. Contact: support@mkweli.tech.

How do I verify the download is genuine?

Download only from https://aml.mkweli.tech/. Current file: Mkweli_v1.0.12.apk, package com.mkwelimobile. Compare the SHA-256 in the download section of this page with the file you received.

Can we use it alongside our existing vendor?

Yes. Many teams use Mkweli as a mobile / offline complement to core systems — for branches, agents, or contingency screening.
